# Morph Lightbox

## Purpose

`MorphLightbox` expands a selected photograph or media thumbnail into a focused collection view while preserving its spatial identity. Use it for short photography, portfolio and product galleries.

Supply useful labels, alt text and captions; keep collections small enough for deliberate previous/next inspection. Avoid generic modal content, huge feeds or opening unrelated media. Arrow controls, Escape, backdrop close, focus trap/restoration and scroll lock must remain intact.

Reduced motion opens immediately and touch users receive explicit controls. Lazy-load gallery media and do not preload every video. Use `ExpandableMedia` when the detail should remain structurally inside the article.
